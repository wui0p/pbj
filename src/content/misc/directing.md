Yes, sort of. I directed a short film (20+ minutes) in high school. It was a group project for Chinese class, with six of us on the team, and I was the director, main scriptwriter, and main producer. As the producer, I rented our school on a Saturday as the filming location and brought everything we needed for the shoot, like computers, bags, and parts for a mini robot car.

The film is called <span lang="zh-Hant">《座位》</span> (“Seats”), and it’s about friendship at school. It’s easy to watch. The camera stays in one place the whole time, and the frame shows only four seats. Everything in this story happens in those four seats. Our teacher said, “I thought it would be boring when I saw the script, but it is surprisingly interesting and funny.”
