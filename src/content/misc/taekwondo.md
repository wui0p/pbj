order: 1

I trained in taekwondo (<span lang="zh-Hant">跆拳道</span>) for more than four years and reached the red-black belt (1<sup>st</sup> gup, one level below black belt). I stopped after high school, but I still remember those days. Back then, I hated it when my father took me to practice every week, mostly because I didn’t enjoy the sport and because the dobok (the taekwondo uniform) was so itchy. I didn’t understand why my parents wanted me to learn such a niche sport.

Now, I’m thankful to my parents for (partly) forcing me to train every week. Even though I still don’t really enjoy the sport, I’m happy to have this experience, and at least I know how to protect myself. I did enjoy training with the other students/friends: sparring with them and celebrating together after belt tests.
