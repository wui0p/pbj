Yes. I designed every page before writing any code. Each page started as a slide, where I designed where the words go, which colors and paintings to use, and how things move as you scroll. On every slide, I wrote notes to explain exactly what I wanted and used AI tools to turn those slides into the real website. With each change or new idea, I noted it in the slides and made a new version.

I’m not a website designer, so I went online (YouTube, websites, or blogs) to see what UI and UX engineers do on their own portfolio websites to show off their work. The earlier versions had some bad UX and design choices. For example, the main page had too much information and didn’t show my personality without relying on words.
