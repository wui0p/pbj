I know it may not sound grand, but I’m proud of planning [entire trips to Japan](../travel/) with three of my high school friends, none of whom had been to Japan. It took many meetings, constant communication, and adjusting plans to fit everyone’s preferences and schedules. I wrote a full travel guidebook and schedule, so they would know where we were going and could share feedback and opinions before we left.

What really made me proud wasn’t the planning itself, but watching my friends enjoy the trip and have fun. It’s the same thing I value in teaching: seeing others thrive and be happy through my efforts. I felt fulfilled watching my friends laugh and discover new things throughout the trip. For me, I get to see new places that I normally wouldn’t go to by myself and chat with them when I’m bored.
