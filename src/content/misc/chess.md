Chess is a very interesting game. I started playing in my junior year of undergrad, knowing almost nothing about it (not even the rules). My highest rating is 1050 on Chess.com. That isn’t a high rating, but chess is just a kind of hobby for me, so it is good enough. Maybe I’ll climb even higher in the future.

My favorite opening is the Ponziani Opening. It looks quiet and like a normal beginner opening, but if my opponent plays the natural moves, there are several traps they can fall into. Even if they know the Ponziani and avoid them all, I can keep playing my normal opening moves without hurting the overall structure much. So, it is a versatile way to start playing with a few traps along the way, and I enjoy the simplicity of it (if there is a problem, just abort).
