I guided and trained students from Taichung Municipal Taichung First Senior High School, preparing them to serve effectively as educational volunteers for the “Physics World” exhibition project in the National Museum, a collaborative initiative sponsored and supported by the Delta Electronics Foundation, the Taichung First Senior High School Alumni Association, and the National Museum of Natural Science. My main objective was to coordinate exhibition timelines and mentor the volunteer teams as the floor supervisor.
