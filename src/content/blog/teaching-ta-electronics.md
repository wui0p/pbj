Typically, undergraduate students in my major are not allowed to serve as teaching assistants. However, based on my performance and dedication to securing this role, I was accepted as the TA for the Electronics (II) course. In this role, I authored original problem sets and solution manuals. I worked closely with the professor and the other teaching assistants to fix an issue in a solution. Before the midterm, I held a TA session to help the undergraduates (mainly sophomores) understand confusing concepts.
